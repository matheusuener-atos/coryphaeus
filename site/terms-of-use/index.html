1. What PAULUS is

PAULUS is a Windows desktop program that works as an artificial-intelligence legal assistant. It runs on the office's machine, with a local AI model (Ollama), and brings together a document archive, editor, calendar, digital signatures, email, contacts, finances and an Approvals queue.

Documents, messages and answers stay on your computer; no PAULUS server receives them. The program checks the paulus.ia.br website for new versions and, on the Open development screen, reads the public history; if you support the project, the website passes the payment to Mercado Pago. For email and Google services, the program connects directly to your provider (Google, Microsoft or other).

3. The professional's responsibility

The assistant suggests; you decide. PAULUS does not provide legal advice and can be wrong. Reviewing and deciding on each document, deadline, reply and submission is up to the professional using the program.

  • Check every answer against the original document and current law before using it.
  • Deadlines suggested by the program (for example, found in contracts or emails) are suggestions: confirm the count and the date before relying on them.
  • Sensitive actions — such as sending an email, signing a document or moving and deleting files — go through the Approvals queue and only happen with a person's confirmation. Whoever approves is responsible for what they approved.
  • You are responsible for having a legal basis to process the client and third-party data you put into the program, and for the professional secrecy that applies to it.

6. Remote access (optional)

The office can turn on remote access, on its own computer, to use PAULUS from home or a phone. It comes turned off. When on:

  • PAULUS and the documents stay on the office computer, which must be on with the program open; what travels over the internet is the screen;
  • the route is a Cloudflare tunnel, at an <office>.paulus.ia.br address created in the project's Cloudflare account. Midway, traffic passes unencrypted through Cloudflare; we do not route, inspect or log that content (see the Privacy Policy);
  • the office is responsible for the accounts it creates and the security level it chooses for each one (every person passes an anti-bot check and signs in with their own account and, depending on that level, an authenticator code), for keeping recovery codes, and for turning off or removing access when no longer needed;
  • signing, moving or deleting files, exporting the archive and changing settings remain only on the office computer; what is proposed remotely in the calendar, tasks and contacts goes through the Approvals queue;
  • every access is logged on the office computer;
  • the paulus.ia.br address is offered while there are slots in this phase, with no guarantee of continuous availability; an address never connected within 7 days, or idle for more than 180, is released automatically.
