PAVLVS — AI legal assistant

The whole law office, with an AI that stays on your machine.

PAVLVS (PAULUS Legal) is a Windows program for Brazilian law offices. It brings together the office's documents, email, calendar, recordings, finances and an AI assistant. The AI is part of a subscription and runs in the PAULUS cloud: only the question and the excerpts it needs are sent, with personal data masked, and the office's files stay on its own computer. Without internet, a local model answers as a backup.

No account or sign-in is needed to read this page or to download the program. The program is in Portuguese; this page explains it in English.

What PAVLVS does

  • Archive (Acervo): reads the office's PDFs, Word and Excel files in the folders you choose, and answers questions about them, citing the page.
  • Email: your inbox inside the program; the assistant drafts replies, and nothing is sent without your approval.
  • Calendar and deadlines: appointments, tasks, procedural deadlines counted in business days, and court publications for your bar number.
  • Recordings: records a meeting, transcribes it locally and turns the action items into tasks.
  • Services, clients, finances and signatures: the office's cases, contacts, billable hours and digital signatures in one place.
  • Approvals: the AI proposes, you approve. Anything that leaves the machine — an email, a document sent to Drive — waits for your yes.

Your documents stay where they are

PAVLVS is installed on the office computer, where documents are read and indexed. The AI is part of a subscription: with it, answers are written in the cloud, which only turns on after the office's principal says yes — through the PAULUS plan (the question and the excerpts it needs pass through paulus.ia.br to DeepInfra, which runs the model, and are not stored) or with the office's own DeepInfra, Anthropic or OpenAI API key, with CPF, CNPJ and case numbers masked and nothing from email. Without internet, or when the month's quota runs out, a local model running through Ollama answers as a backup; without the subscription, the program works without AI. No PAVLVS server keeps your messages, documents or client data.

Why PAVLVS asks for access to your Google account

When you connect a Gmail account to the Email module, Google shows the consent screen of the app PAVLVS — Assistente jurídico de IA local. PAVLVS requests the account's email address and access to Gmail (scope https://mail.google.com/) only to show your inbox and the messages you open, to suggest calendar deadlines found in messages, and to send the replies you approve.

Optional, each one only when you connect it in Settings › Connections:

  • Google Calendar (scope calendar.events): copy your appointments there — title, date, time, duration and place — and create their Google Meet room.
  • Google Drive, upload (scope drive.file, which only sees what PAVLVS created): upload the documents you approve to a PAULUS folder in your Drive.
  • Google Drive, read-only (scope drive.readonly): let you choose Drive folders and keep a local copy of them in the Archive, so you can search those documents on your computer. PAVLVS never writes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive with this access.

Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models and not shared with third parties. PAVLVS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

You can turn it off at any time: remove the account in PAVLVS and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Download

PAVLVS is open-source software for Windows; the AI assistant is by subscription. Download the installer from the project's latest release on GitHub; the source code is at github.com/matheusuener-atos/coryphaeus.
