Four rules that do not change.

01

The files stay here.

Documents, the index and the backup model stay on the office's machine. Without internet, a local model answers.

02

Nothing leaves without your yes.

Sending email, signing, and moving or deleting files go through the Approvals queue before they happen.

03

Sources for everything.

An answer about your documents shows the file, the page and the passage it came from. The model can be wrong: the source is there for you to check.

04

Pay only for the AI.

The program works in full without paying; the AI is by subscription.

The AI proposes. You approve.

Emails, signatures and file moves go into a queue. You see what will go out, whether it can be undone, and decide.

Record the meeting. Action items become tasks.

Transcription on this machine, with the minute of each passage. The AI writes the summary from the text, and the action items become Calendar tasks. None of the audio leaves the computer.

Your documents stay where they are.

The files stay on the office's computer. Only the question and the excerpts the AI needs to read are sent, with CPF, CNPJ and case numbers masked, and the text is not stored anywhere else. No server of ours keeps your documents or messages.

Why PAULUS asks for access to your Google account.

When you connect a Gmail account to the Email module, Google shows the consent screen of the app PAVLVS — Assistente jurídico de IA local. PAULUS requests the account's email address and access to Gmail (https://mail.google.com/) for three things only: to show your inbox and the messages you open, which come straight from Gmail to your computer; to turn deadlines found in messages into Calendar suggestions; and to prepare and send the replies you approve, through the Approvals queue. Archiving, deleting, marking as read or starred are also your actions.

Optional, each one only when you connect it in Settings › Connections: Google Calendar (calendar.events), to copy your appointments there and create their Google Meet room; Google Drive (drive.file, which only sees what PAULUS created), to upload the documents you approve to a PAULUS folder; and Drive read-only (drive.readonly), to keep in the Library a local copy of the folders you choose.

Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models and not shared with third parties. The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The authorization is encrypted by your Windows account. To turn it off, remove the account in PAULUS and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the Privacy policy and the Terms of use.

Install, point to the folder, ask.

The installer brings PAULUS and, if missing, Ollama, the engine of the local backup model. On first launch, the setup assistant tests the machine and suggests the local model that fits it. The AI comes with the subscription: subscribe here and sign in to PAULUS with the same Google account, in Settings › Models. Without it, the program works in full, without AI.
